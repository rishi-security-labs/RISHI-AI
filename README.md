# ⚡ RISHI BOT v2.0 — WhatsApp AI & Multi-Device Bot

An advanced, fast, and lightweight WhatsApp Multi-Device bot powered by **Baileys** and **Groq AI**.

---

## ✨ Features
- 🤖 **Smart AI Chat:** Auto-reply in DMs with conversation memory.
- 🔓 **Anti-ViewOnce (`.vv`):** Unlock view-once photos & videos easily.
- 🎨 **Sticker Maker (`.s`):** Convert any image into a WhatsApp sticker.
- 🗣️ **Text-to-Speech (`.tts`):** Convert text into Hindi/English voice notes.
- 🛡️ **Group Management:** Includes `.tagall`, `.kick`, and `.antilink on/off`.
- 👁️ **Auto-Status View:** Automatically views contacts' WhatsApp statuses.
- ⚙️ **Live Controls:** Switch `.mode public/private`, `.autoreply`, and `.autoreact` directly from WhatsApp.
